Low-Value Guard
About & guide

YOUR FEED, REFINED

Make your feed work for you.

Low-Value Guard checks LinkedIn posts and blurs or hides the ones it classifies as low value. You can use the shared classifier right away or teach a personal model from your own feedback.

01

Start with the shared classifier

Open the extension popup and turn on Feed filtering. You can start without an account. Choose Blur to reveal a filtered post with “Show post,” or Hide to remove it from view.

02

Teach it what matters to you

  1. Sign in or create an account in the popup.
  2. At the bottom of a post, choose Useful or Not useful to save your feedback.
  3. Use Count my saved feedback to check your progress. We recommend saving at least 1,500 posts, with both Useful and Not useful examples, before training.
  4. Click Train my model in the popup. Training starts in the background; it can take time to finish.

Your account keeps up to 3,000 distinct feedback posts. When you save a new post beyond that limit, the oldest saved post is removed. Changing a saved post’s label does not move it to the end.

03

What happens behind the scenes

Your feedback saves the text of each labeled post and your Useful or Not useful choice to your account. When you request training, a convolutional neural network (CNN) learns from all your saved feedback. Once your personal model is available, it classifies posts on your feed. The shared classifier continues to work while your model is being trained.
