Low-Value Guard
About & guide

STILL IN DEVELOPMENT

If you are interested, check out the repo

This extension is in development. It classifies LinkedIn posts and blurs or hides posts labeled Not useful. It supports a shared classifier and personal models trained on saved feedback.

https://github.com/Phuong-Le-NN/linkedin_training

01

Use the shared classifier

Open the extension popup and turn on Feed filtering. You can start without an account. Choose Blur to reveal a filtered post with “Show post,” or Hide to remove it from view.

02

Save feedback and train a model

  1. Sign in or create an account in the popup.
  2. At the bottom of a post, choose Useful or Not useful to save your feedback.
  3. Use Count my saved feedback to see how many posts are saved. We recommend saving at least 1,500 posts, with both Useful and Not useful examples, before training.
  4. Click Train my model in the popup. Training starts in the background; it can take time to finish.

Your account keeps up to 3,000 distinct feedback posts. When you save a new post beyond that limit, the oldest saved post is removed. Changing a saved post’s label does not move it to the end.

03

Classification and training

Saving feedback stores the post text and its Useful or Not useful label under your account. Training uses all saved feedback to train a convolutional neural network (CNN). Once the personal model is available, it is used for classification. The shared classifier is used until then.
